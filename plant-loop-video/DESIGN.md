# Indoor Botanical Cinema

## Style Prompt

Dark, cinematic indoor cultivation photography. The room and camera remain completely fixed while a restrained breeze moves only the living canopy. Natural botanical detail takes priority over graphic effects.

## Colors

- Room black: `#070907`
- Charcoal: `#171a16`
- Leaf green: `#456b22`
- Highlight green: `#91a84a`
- Grow-light white: `#f5eed5`

## Motion

Six-second seamless breathing loop. Low-amplitude organic deformation is isolated to the lower plant canopy; no camera movement, zoom, or background drift.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not move the room or camera.
- Do not use neon or purple grow lighting.
- Do not add text, logos, UI, or people.
- Do not make the plants rubbery or liquid.
